Transmutations

a text-sound poem by David Braden

A generative text-sound poem that begins with a random set of phonemes, which gradually rearrange themselves to align with the phonetic style of a poet (in this case the sprung rhythm of Gerard Manley Hopkins) before crystallizing into words. After the words are reshuffled for poetic cohesion, the poetic line begins to decay again into a new set of phonemes in an endless cycle.
